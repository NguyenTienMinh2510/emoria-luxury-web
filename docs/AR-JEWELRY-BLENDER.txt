REVISION: SIMPLE SIZING / RESPONSIVE TRACKING (2026-09-24)
This section supersedes the historical revision notes below.
- Removed card/ID calibration and screen-ruler workflows from the shared size guide.
  Ring: inside diameter of an existing well-fitting ring, using a real ruler;
  wrist: soft tape; necklace: a cord cut to the desired chain length.
  Circumference = pi * inside diameter. No inferred national ring-size mapping.
- AR v4 uses world-landmark estimates in metres to estimate pixels/mm. Median
  hand-bone ratios reduce local landmark noise. Without world data the old
  anatomical scale is retained as an approximation, including 360 mm shoulders.
  A customer correction of 80–120% is available under Fine-tune the fit.
- Ring inner diameter follows selected mm; band width is 3.8 mm from catalog
  instead of the source mesh's 5.3 mm. Only the radial plane resizes with size.
  Bracelet base centreline ellipse (29 by 19 mm radii) is approximately 15.25 cm.
- Catalog necklace widths: Lumen/Faith 12 mm, Sen 13 mm. Renderer corrects the
  larger source GLBs without losing the authored chain/bail connection.
  Confirm these against manufactured pieces before claiming physical accuracy.
- Necklace 45/50 cm choices change front-curve arc length; each link and pendant
  retain their dimensions. The unrendered back is an approximate 66 mm radius
  semicircle. Neck shape, clothing occlusion and realistic drape remain estimates.
- Explicit purchase labels like 17 mm or 45 cm update the preview. Numbered sizes,
  ranges and duo labels are not guessed; preview choice never silently changes cart.
- Workers return capture timestamps, world landmarks and hand handedness.
  Request interval reduced from 48 to 30 ms; camera target 30 fps. Rendering
  follows display refresh without the old additional 33 ms gate.
  Motion-responsive smoothing replaces 85 ms smoothing. Translation prediction
  is capped at 45 ms / 12% of tracked span. Results older than 180 ms are hidden.
  The supported necklace turn range still requires both shoulders visible.
- Halo/Echo bevels now have 24 profile vertices rather than 8, with unchanged
  inner diameter; GLBs and Blender roundtrip renders rebuilt. 33,758 / 43,406 tris.
  Lower AR exposure retains more visible metal/enamel surface detail.
- Automated tests: world-to-pixel conversion, fast motion response, prediction
  cap, rear-facing hand orientation, physical size changes, chain arc length,
  fixed link/pendant dimensions, GLB lifecycle, existing API/security checks.
- Browser QA in this revision: desktop and 390 px layout; diameter 17 mm ->
  circumference 53.4 mm; Escape restores the parent AR dialog. Real pose worker
  detected shoulders in the existing public-photo synthetic camera fixture;
  45 -> 50 cm selection worked, capture produced a local blob, and camera tracks
  ended on close. No horizontal overflow at 390 px. Physical phone latency,
  physical fit and production deployment are not verified.
- Measurement references:
  https://www.gia.edu/quality-assurance-benchmark/accurate-determination-finger-ring-size
  https://www.tiffany.ae/en-ae/size-guide
  https://developers.google.com/edge/mediapipe/solutions/vision/hand_landmarker/web_js

REVISION: FIXED NECKLACES, 3D SIDE VIEWS, ORBIT
Current assets: 8 GLBs including emoria-orbit.glb (blue/gold bracelet, duo previews one).
Orbit reference: public/ar/orbit.jpg. Chain reference: public/ar/chain.jpg.
Faith/Lumen/Sen GLBs now contain an authored ChainAnchor Empty in the bail opening.
Renderer uses this anchor and the same 3D transform for both chain and pendant.
Chain: instanced oval cable links, approximately 2.98 mm outer width / 0.88 mm wire diameter.
Lumen: extra stone claws; all necklace bails have denser rounded geometry and a true depth opening.
PBR enamel/pearl/gemstone properties are preserved, never indiscriminately converted to metallic silver.
Necklace length, pendant scale and vertical-position controls have been removed from the customer UI.
Lumen face 28 mm; Faith cross approximately 18 x 29 mm; Sen approximately 26 mm across.
These modeled dimensions are inferred from images, NOT verified against physical products.
Only the visible front chain is rendered, styled as a 45 cm necklace.
Pixel scale uses an assumed 360 mm shoulder span; camera alone cannot measure actual shoulder width.
REST version 3 config is shared in src/services/tryOnConfig.js, re-exported by server/tryOn.js.
Necklaces return fixedDimensions and shoulderReferenceMm. No database migration needed.
Custom GLBs should include ChainAnchor; bounding-box fallback for old custom models needs visual review.

Pose worker supplies world landmarks for shoulder yaw and (when hips are reliably visible) torso pitch.
Quaternion smoothing and foreshortening compensation maintain consistent size during moderate turns.
Estimated yaw up to ~66 degrees is supported while both shoulders remain detectable.
When hips are out of frame, pitch stays neutral. This is not full 360-degree tracking.
Hair/clothing/body occlusion and chain physics remain approximations.
AR WebGL supersampling is capped at 1.5x; camera render target remains ~30 fps.
Tests now cover all 8 GLBs, anchors over 3D rotations/distances, fixed scale, preserved enamel,

REST / RUNTIME
GET /api/v1/products/:id/try-on verifies the product is active, returns the matching GLB and calibration.
src/services/tryOnConfig.js: curated 8 designs, supported duo aliases, optional details.ar overrides.
Model URLs must be same-origin .glb paths. enabled:false disables AR.
Products without a matching 3D asset no longer display an unrelated generic design.
Horizon is a bracelet; Halo/Echo are rings; Lumen/Faith/Sen use necklace placement.
For Sen this is a charm suspended on a simulated chain, not a claim that a chain is included in the order.
GLB meters; +Y up, +Z ornament front; ring hole axis Y. Dimensions are inferred, not measured.
Necklace chain uses fixed link geometry, scaled with the same camera calibration as its pendant.
Ring/wrist depth masks are geometric approximations. No hair/clothing segmentation.
Hand orientation is optimized for a frontal view; the same model is displayed on either hand.

PERFORMANCE
Only the selected GLB is loaded when camera starts; Three.js remains in lazy AR chunks.
~0.29–1.88 MB per GLB, roughly 10,400–65,600 triangles.
Meshes joined by material; necklace links use one instanced draw and are not rebuilt during tracking.
Camera rendering capped around 30 fps; pose tracking runs in a worker, requested no faster than every 48 ms.
Model scale responds to landmarks. Only ring/bracelet types retain size/placement controls.
Camera tracks, workers, GPU buffers, environment maps and photo blob URLs released on close.
Late model/camera results are discarded after close; missing GLB/WebGL produces a visible error.

VISUAL LIMITS
These are modeled interpretations of single reference photos, not exact scans or manufacturing CAD.
Back sides, depth, chain wrap and dimensions are inferred. Preview is not a ring-size measurement.
Crystal material is a real-time approximation; it does not simulate diamond caustics or spectral dispersion.
Desktop browser and synthetic video tests do not establish tracking accuracy or frame rate on a physical iPhone.
