This is an authored visual reconstruction, not a scan or manufacturing CAD.
Diameter 28 mm and thickness are provisional. Back is plain inferred silver.
Sun rays, pave layout and bail are approximations to the available image.
No physical material composition or precise gemstone cut is certified.

GLB uses meters, front +Z and up +Y. Origin is disk center.
Bail anchor for AR: approximately (0, 0.018, 0) meters.
Necklace file includes a separate static display chain, not a physically sized,
rigged or simulated necklace. AR must deform/replace it to match body tracking.
No catalog, database, deployment or AR routing is changed by this build.

Four PBR materials: silver, gold, black enamel, crystal.
Lighting/environment is required in Three.js for realistic metal reflections.
GLB uses transmission/IOR-related extensions when exported by Blender;
verify support in the target renderer, particularly on mobile.
Studio lights, background and camera are saved in BLEND but excluded from GLB.
The script joins by material to reduce draw calls and reports triangle counts.

Before real sales use: confirm dimensions and rear design, review the visual
match, calibrate scale/anchors, and test hand/body occlusion on real devices.
